# Analysis and Outcomes

This section covers analysis and Outcomes. It develops service Indicators, Objectives, Agreements And Budgets through mechanisms, examples, prerequisites and failure reasoning.

Read the module goals, teaching and closing summary in order. YAML checks are indexed separately and appear after designated summaries. Larger domains can include intermediate checkpoints before the final review.

## Learning Outcomes

- Distinguish an SLI, SLO and SLA.
- Define good and valid event populations and an evaluation window.
- Calculate an error budget and interpret burn rate.
- Account for low traffic, exclusions and incomplete observation.
- Choose panels for rate, duration, errors and saturation.
- Explain time range, resolution and units.
- Preserve grouping and denominator context.
- Distinguish display gaps from measured zero and alert evaluation.
